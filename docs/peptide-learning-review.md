Key distinctions:

- PT-141: defined HSDD population and subcutaneous product versus male use, performance claims and nasal formulations. The mechanism of clinical benefit remains incompletely understood. Relevant label contraindications and interactions are included.

- TB-500 versus thymosin beta-4; CJC DAC versus other preparations; kisspeptin-10 versus -54; NAD+ versus its precursors remain distinct.
- BPC-157: uncontrolled pain observations are not proof of structural healing. No binder dosing schedules or broad efficacy tiers were reproduced.

Not a systematic review: the update provides study-linked educational context. It does not claim exhaustive literature coverage. Numeric outcome extraction remains limited to directly reviewed trials. Research library automation and paper-level retraction monitoring are separate features.
